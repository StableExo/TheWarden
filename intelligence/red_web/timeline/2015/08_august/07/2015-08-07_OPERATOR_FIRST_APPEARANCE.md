## What We Know

The Red Web operator made their **first documented on-chain deposit at Poloniex** on August 7, 2015 at Block 46,468.

This is significant for two reasons:

1. **KYC record exists.** Poloniex required identity verification. The operator's real identity is on file there — pre-dating the entire network operation by years.

2. **Poloniex ownership changed hands.** Circle sold Poloniex to an anonymous "Asian investor group" in November 2019 — later revealed to be Justin Sun (TRX/TRON, HTX/Huobi). Sun is under SEC civil charges filed March 2023. The IRS/DOJ issued a John Doe summons to Poloniex in 2020. Whether that KYC record was preserved or compromised under Sun's ownership is an open investigative question.
