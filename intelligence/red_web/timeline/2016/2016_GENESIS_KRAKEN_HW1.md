## Why This Matters

Kraken Hot Wallet 1 (NODE_009) is one of the **primary subpoena targets** in the Red Web investigation. It processed hundreds of millions in ETH flows connected to the network. The fact that two dedicated genesis wallets were the *first* to touch it in 2016 — and both are still active a decade later — points to a deliberate, long-running relationship with the exchange, not opportunistic use.

Kraken is a US-regulated exchange (FinCEN registered, CFTC-registered). Any transactions flowing through Kraken HW1 are subject to US subpoena power. NODE_038 and NODE_039 represent the beginning of that traceable, legally accessible record.
