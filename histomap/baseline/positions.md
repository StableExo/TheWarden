# Baseline positions

Running record of each participant's stance on the three baseline questions, and the open forks that follow. Update as replies arrive.

## eesel (2026-11-26, v0.1; updated after Gemini)

- Criteria: MODIFY. One power test instead of a checklist; drop writing as a criterion; represent the non-state majority.
- Width: initially population-primary (area as estimator). After Gemini: accept a two-term composite (land + population), resisting a subjective "complexity" term.
- Start: origin ~3200 BCE. First slice: proposed 3500-3000, then conceded to 3200-2800 (Caral's monumental phase is ~3000, so it falls inside this slice).

## Gemini (2026-11-26)

- Criteria: AGREE, with (a) a nomadic/pastoral confederation exception ("OR a nomadic/pastoral confederation exercising sustained military and territorial hegemony across a major macro-region") and (b) a minimum width floor of ~1-2% of active world share, with sub-threshold polities bundled into regional cultural blocs (e.g. "Levantine City-States", "Mesoamerican Polities").
- Width: MODIFY to a weighted composite, "Weighted Geopolitical and Demographic Share": e.g. 50/50 Controlled Habitable Land + Share of Global Population, or 40/40/20 adding economic-urban complexity.
- Start: AGREE. Origin 3200-3000 BCE, first working slice 3200-2800 BCE.

## Open forks to resolve

1. Width formula: pure land vs weighted composite, and whether to add a complexity term.
2. Minimum stream-width floor: yes/no, value (Gemini proposes 1-2%), and how sub-threshold polities bundle.
3. Non-state majority: neutral "other" band vs width defined as state-controlled share only. Note: under population weighting this band may dominate the early slices.
4. Scope: the connected world growing over time, vs all qualifying societies everywhere from antiquity.
5. Overlap/contestation convention (nomads, frontiers, tributary zones).
6. Naming convention (stable cultural streams with polity sub-labels).
7. Sources and confidence standard.
