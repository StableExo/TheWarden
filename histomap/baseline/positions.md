## Claude (2026-11-26)

- Criteria: MODIFY. One power test instead of a checklist; drop writing as a criterion; represent the non-state majority. Converged with the group.
- Width: population-primary, moved to a two-term composite (controlled land + population), resisting a subjective "complexity" term except as a documented tiebreaker.

## Gemini (2026-11-26)

- Criteria: AGREE, adding (a) a nomadic/pastoral confederation exception and (b) a minimum width floor ~1-2% with sub-threshold polities bundled into regional cultural blocs.
- Width: MODIFY to a weighted composite (controlled habitable land + share of global population; optionally adding economic-urban complexity).
- Start: AGREE on 3200-3000 BCE origin, first slice 3200-2800 BCE.

## Grok (2026-11-26)

- Criteria: AGREE with light tightening. Require systemic complexity beyond a single village; soft floor ~1-2% before a stream gets its own colour; unit = cultural-political blocs, not ethnic labels.
- Width: AGREE that width = the land (and populations/power on it) controlled or contested by societies meeting the criteria. Empty/unorganized zones take no width. Primary metric: controlled territory weighted by agricultural productivity + estimated population under control.
- Start: AGREE. ~3200-3000 BCE origin, first slice 3200-2800 BCE.

## Muse AI (Meta) (2026-11-26)

- Criteria: MODIFY / AGREE WITH AMENDMENTS. Formalised as a test: 1 + 2 + 4 AND (3a writing OR 3b administrative complexity), with the criteria made concrete. Two additions: (a) grouping rule - city-states count as one bloc (Sumer is one stream in 3200-2800 BCE); split only when a polity unifies >50% of the bloc; (b) residual rule - explicit "Other peoples" streams from day one, two for Slice 1: "Other settled farming peoples" and "Other pastoralist / mobile peoples".
- Width: AGREE with clarification. 100% = controlled/contested habitable land, denominator expands over time, width = share of controlled productive/habitable land area, NOT population (population goes in the notes). Estimates as ranges. Contested land counted once, assigned to the dominant controller. Accepts early states are thin slivers and the residual dominates, probably 85%+ in the first slice.
- Start: AGREE. Start at 3200 BCE, first slice 3200-2800 BCE. Test roster for Slice 1: Sumer, Egypt, Proto-Elamite / Elam, Early Harappan, plus the two Other residuals; China (Longshan/Yangshao), Norte Chico and Mesoamerica stay in Other for this slice.
