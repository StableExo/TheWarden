## 4. Method notes

- **Evidence rule:** every width comes from the integrated core. Harappan and Longshan are not counted by artifact distribution.
- **Time-varying width:** used for Harappan (ramp) and Elam (decline). Everything else is flat across the slice.
- **Floor is not an inclusion rule:** Caral passes and is drawn as a labelled sliver, not bundled into Other settled. Longshan and Corded Ware fail on the test, not on size.
- **Sumer treated as a system:** answered by the charter's grouping rule - city-states count as one bloc, so Sumer (ED II-III) is one stream, not several. If split, each city-state would sit at or under the floor.
