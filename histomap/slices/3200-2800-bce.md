## How the numbers are built

- Width = controlled productive land x productivity weight, normalised; the inputs reconcile to the published widths.
- Centrals sum to 100%; ranges are uncertainty around each central, not independent ranges.
- **Population cross-check:** bracket, do not pick one. Test whether the *ordering* of streams survives at both a low anchor (McEvedy and Jones, ~14M world at 3000 BCE) and a high one (HYDE, ~45M). Shares shift between anchors; the ordering is the real test.
- **Denominator gutter:** state what the "relevant world" includes each slice, and show the absolute total, so a share falling because new regions were added is not mistaken for real decline. Denominator = **land used for farming or herding**. Pure forager territory is outside the width, shown as a labelled "not counted" note in the gutter; regions enter the denominator when they adopt farming or herding, flagged there.

## Streams: inclusion tests (all logged, no assumed passes)

- **Sumer, Egypt:** settle-state / city-network with systemic complexity. PASS.
- **Proto-Elamite / Elam:** SUSIANA core with an administrative record system (proto-cuneiform, seals); political unity debated but the administrative complexity and bounded core territory clear the test. PASS (logged).
- **Liangzhu:** rubric written before the test (settlement-size hierarchy; large-scale labour mobilisation; bounded territory). PASS - ~300 ha walled city plus 100+ satellite sites, ~100 km2 engineered hydraulics and monumental works, a core of settled territory under central authority. No writing required.

## Folded and logged (break-out triggers)

- **Early Harappan:** FOLDED (unanimous). A Regionalisation-Era material horizon (Ravi / Kot Diji), spread not control. Folded as a labelled, hatched regional sub-band inside "Other settled farming peoples" ("**Indus-Baluchistan farming complex**"), so it stays visible. Break-out trigger: the Mature Harappan (~2600 BCE).
- **Yamnaya:** FAIL for its own stream (a kurgan/chiefdom horizon, not a confederation with effective control; Gemini, Muse, Sonnet). Stays inside the pastoral residual with a named note; the first clear pastoral pass is probably the Scythian/Xiongnu era.
- **Caral-Supe (Norte Chico, Peru):** monumental construction begins ~3000 BCE, main phase mostly ~2600 BCE onward. Stays in the residual; breaks out later.
- **Late Yangshao / Early Longshan (Yellow River):** walled settlements from ~3000 BCE, large centres later. Stays in the residual; break-out date to settle (Gemini: Slice 2; Sonnet: hold longer).
