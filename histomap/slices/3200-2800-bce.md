## Scope (regions in play this era)

The connected complex-society world of the late 4th millennium BCE: southern Mesopotamia, the Nile valley, the Iranian plateau fringe, and the Indus basin. Other regions with emerging complexity are tracked inside the residuals (see below) rather than as standalone streams.

## Residual notes

The "Other settled farming peoples" residual is expected to dominate this slice (roughly 85%+ of the width). It should explicitly document why two emerging centres stay inside the residual here rather than breaking out as streams, so the inclusion bar is applied consistently across the world and the slice is not read as Old World bias:

- **Caral-Supe (Norte Chico, Peru):** monumental complexes emerging around 3000 BCE, so they fall late inside this window. Tracked in the residual for Slice 1; a candidate to break out once it clearly passes the full test in a later slice.
- **Late Yangshao / Early Longshan (Yellow River basin):** laying the urban groundwork, not yet clearly past the test this slice. Tracked in the residual.
