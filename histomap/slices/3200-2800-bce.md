# Slice 1: 3200-2800 BCE

**Version:** 1, with Claude Sonnet 5.5's review folded in (2026-11-26). Awaiting final confirmation and a decision on Early Harappan.
**Metric:** controlled productive land weighted by estimated agricultural productivity; population as a cross-check.

## Method note

- **Centrals sum to 100%; ranges are uncertainty around each central**, not independent ranges.
- **Land x weight reconciles to the widths** (see the check below).
- **Population cross-check: bracket, do not pick one.** Test whether the ratios survive at both a low anchor (McEvedy and Jones, ~14M world at 3000 BCE) and a high one (HYDE, ~45M). If the ratios hold at both ends they are robust; if not, we flag it.
- **Denominator gutter.** State what the "relevant world" includes each slice, and draw a thin gutter showing the absolute total, so a stream's share falling because new regions were added is not mistaken for real decline.

## Reconciliation check (land x weight vs width)

- Sumer: 35-55k x 0.9 = 31.5-49.5k weighted.
- Egypt: 25-40k x 1.25 = 31-50k weighted.
- Elam: 15-25k x 0.6 = 9-15k weighted.
- Harappan: 60-120k x 0.5 = 30-60k weighted.

## Open decisions (opened by Sonnet's review)

- **Early Harappan: keep or fold?** Its land implies ~1.6x Sumer's, but in 3200-2800 BCE it shows shared material culture rather than clear political control. Sonnet: cut it to ~1.5-2%, or fold it into the settled residual until the Mature Harappan (~2600 BCE). Muse had already called it the weakest named stream. Needs a team call.
- **Liangzhu (Yangtze delta, c. 3300-2300 BCE):** walled city and large water-control works, contemporary with this slice. Test against the inclusion rule now, and log pass or fail so the rule is visibly falsifiable.
- **Yamnaya (Pontic-Caspian steppe):** test on the pastoral side, and log the result.
- **Foragers:** the two residuals cover settled farming and pastoral peoples only. Do non-farming, non-pastoral foragers get a band, or are they excluded (the land metric makes their land near-zero regardless)?
- **Forward handoffs to plan now:** the metric (land-only increasingly understates industrial powers such as Britain and the US after ~1800) and the unit (the 1931 chart flips from civilisations to nation-groups near the present).

## Pastoral floor and sensitivity

- Floor kept at 0.2, with a **sensitivity band of 0.1-0.3** (Sonnet). Without the floor the pastoral stream roughly halves.
- **Weight-spread check:** Egypt 1.25 vs farmers 0.5 vs herders 0.2 may be compressed against density evidence, and compression inflates the residuals. To verify against the sources below.

## Residual notes

- **Caral-Supe (Norte Chico, Peru):** monumental construction begins around 3000 BCE but the main phase is mostly ~2600 BCE onward. Stays in the residual for Slice 1; breaks out later. (Sonnet agrees on Caral.)
- **Late Yangshao / Early Longshan (Yellow River):** many walled settlements from ~3000 BCE, large centres later. Sonnet would hold the Yellow River longer than Gemini's Slice 2; settle the break-out date for both.
- **Liangzhu** is the case to test for this slice on the settled side (above), rather than Caral or the Yellow River.
