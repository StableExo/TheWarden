# Slice 1: 3200-2800 BCE

**Status:** DRAFT, revised after Gemini's review (2026-11-26). Grok's and Muse's reviews are pending. Metric locked: controlled productive land weighted by estimated agricultural productivity; population as a cross-check; raw area as the fallback. Every figure is a reasoned order-of-magnitude estimate given as a range, not a measurement.

## Scope (regions in play this era)

The connected complex-society world of the late 4th millennium BCE: southern Mesopotamia, the Nile valley, the Iranian plateau fringe, and the Indus basin. Other regions with emerging complexity are tracked inside the residuals (see below) rather than as standalone streams.

## Streams

**Combined:** the four named states total about **13-19%** (central ~16%); the two residuals about **81-87%**.

## Decisions from review

- **Sumer nudged from 3-6% to 4-7%** (Gemini): irrigated southern Mesopotamian alluvium was at least on par with the Nile in area and yield, so Sumer should not sit below Egypt. Alternative if we prefer: hold 3-6% and raise Sumer's weight to 1.05-1.1. Open to Grok and Muse.
- **Egypt, Proto-Elamite / Elam, Early Harappan ranges confirmed** by Gemini.
- **Proto-Elamite / Elam kept as one stream** in Slice 1, to avoid a sub-1% sliver; the highland-lowland network transition (Susiana, Anshan/Tal-i Malyan, Sialk, Yahya) is noted in the stream notes.
- **Early Harappan's width reflects broad cultural-settlement reach** (Kot Diji / Ravi / Hakra phases) rather than tight imperial control; centralised administration only arrives with the Mature Harappan (~2600 BCE).
- **Pastoral weighting floor kept at 0.2** (Gemini): Yamnaya, Afanasievo and early Saharan/desert pastoralists actively controlled and contested vast zones with early wheel and horse mobility. Zeroing them out would break our own control/contestation rule and the continuity into later steppe streams.
- **Caral-Supe and Yellow River (Late Yangshao / Early Longshan) stay inside the settled residual for Slice 1**, documented below; both are promoted to standalone streams in **Slice 2 (2800-2400 BCE)**, when Caral's monumental network and Longshan walled centres (e.g. Taosi, Shimao) hit full stride.

## Residual notes

The "Other settled farming peoples" residual dominates this slice (roughly half the width). It documents why two emerging centres stay inside the residual here rather than breaking out as streams, so the inclusion bar is applied consistently across the world and the slice is not read as Old World bias:

- **Caral-Supe (Norte Chico, Peru):** monumental construction at Huaricanga and Caral begins around 3100-3000 BCE, so it covers only the late part of this window and lacks broad regional hegemony yet. Stays in the residual for Slice 1; breaks out in Slice 2.
- **Late Yangshao / Early Longshan (Yellow River basin):** competing walled towns and chiefdoms (Central Plains, Dawenkou/Shandong), not yet a unified bloc or urban state network. Stays in the residual for Slice 1; breaks out in Slice 2.
