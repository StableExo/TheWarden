# Slice 1: 3200-2800 BCE

**Version:** 1 (proposed, 2026-11-26). Reconciled after all three reviews (Gemini, Grok, Muse). Awaiting final confirmation from all four, then locked.
**Metric:** controlled productive land weighted by estimated agricultural productivity; population as a cross-check.

## Method note

- **Centrals sum to 100%; ranges are uncertainty around each central, not independent ranges.** The earlier draft gave overlapping independent ranges, which could total over 100%. Fixed.
- **Land x weight reconciles to the published widths.** Every stream's width is derived from its controlled productive land times its productivity weight, normalised, so the inputs and the widths agree (see the check below).
- **Population cross-check uses McEvedy and Jones** (~14M world population at 3000 BCE). The divergence with other tables (e.g. HYDE at ~45M) is noted, so the check is a sanity band, not a proof.

## Reconciliation check (land x weight vs width)

- Sumer: 35-55k x 0.9 = 31.5-49.5k weighted.
- Egypt: 25-40k x 1.25 = 31-50k weighted.
- Elam: 15-25k x 0.6 = 9-15k weighted.
- Harappan: 60-120k x 0.5 = 30-60k weighted.

Sumer and Egypt now sit close on weighted land, and Egypt's width (5%) sits just above Sumer's (4%), matching its higher productivity weight. Harappan's wide land is discounted by the low weight, so it stays below Egypt.

## Key decisions

- **Numbers trimmed toward Grok and Muse.** Gemini nudged Sumer up; Grok and Muse tightened the named states down. Majority applies: named states 14%, not Gemini's 13-19%. Sumer at 4% sits just under Egypt at 5%, near the parity Gemini wanted but not above it.
- **Egypt's productivity weight raised to 1.2-1.3 and Sumer's lowered to 0.9**, to reconcile the inputs with the widths (the Nile's reliability deserves a bigger gap than 1.0 vs 1.1).
- **Early Harappan trimmed**: 60,000-120,000 km2 of controlled productive land, not 100,000-250,000 of geographic spread; weight 0.5, not 0.6.
- **Proto-Elamite / Elam** trimmed to 15,000-25,000 km2, kept as one stream for Slice 1 (overlap with Sumer in Susiana noted; split in a later slice when Elam becomes a distinct kingdom).
- **Pastoral productivity floor kept at 0.2**, documented explicitly as a pastoral-productivity floor (pastoralism is productive in meat, milk, wool and transport, not only crops). Sensitivity: at floor 0.2 pastoral is 32%; without the floor it roughly halves to ~16% and the settled residual balloons. A 0.15-0.25 band is available for sensitivity testing.
- **Population check anchored to McEvedy and Jones** for Slice 1, divergence noted.
- **Caral-Supe and the Yellow River (Late Yangshao / Early Longshan) stay in the settled residual** for Slice 1 and break out as standalone streams in the next slice, when Caral's monumental network and Longshan walled centres (Taosi) consolidate.

## Residual notes

- **Caral-Supe (Norte Chico, Peru):** neighbouring-valley monumental construction begins around 3000 BCE; Caral itself is late in this window and does not clearly pass the full test in 3200-2800. Stays in the residual; breaks out next slice.
- **Late Yangshao / Early Longshan (Yellow River):** many walled settlements from ~3000 BCE, but the very large centres (>200 ha) develop later in the 3rd millennium. Stays in the residual; breaks out next slice when Taosi and comparable centres consolidate.
