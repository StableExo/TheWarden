## Slice 1 (3200-2800 BCE) - LOCKED (2026-11-26)

**Liangzhu - PASS; thin stream at 1% (range 0.5-1.5%).** Unanimous on the rubric (written before the test): settlement-size hierarchy, large-scale labour mobilisation, bounded territory. Gemini, Grok, Muse, Sonnet, eesel all PASS. Rubric item 3 (central authority) is logged as **inferred** (from site hierarchy and elite burials, as with any pre-writing state). Width taken from the directly evidenced core (the integrated city and hydraulic system), not the wider Liangzhu style zone. Sonnet flags the dates (walls and dams ~3000-2700 BCE), so the stream ramps within the slice rather than sitting flat; verify against UNESCO, the excavation reports and Renfrew & Liu (2018, Antiquity).

**Early Harappan - FOLD.** Unanimous. Folded as the labelled, hatched "Indus-Baluchistan farming complex" sub-band; break-out at the Mature Harappan (~2600 BCE).

**Yamnaya - FAIL its own stream.** Stays in the pastoral residual with a named note.

**Foragers - no band; state the exclusion.** Denominator = farming and herding land; foragers "not counted" in the gutter.

**Proto-Elamite / Elam - PASS (provisional, low confidence).** Administrative record system (Proto-Elelamite tablets, seals, standardisation), multi-settlement hierarchy centred on Susa, surplus extraction; political unity debated; Sumer overlap in Susiana noted.

**Final Slice 1 numbers.** Sumer 4, Egypt 5, Elam 1.5, Liangzhu 1 = 11.5% named; Other settled 56.5% (incl. the hatched sub-band); Other pastoral 32%; sum 100.

**Method rules adopted during this review (now in the charter, v1.2):** evidence rule (width from directly evidenced controlled land, not the style zone); time-varying width within a slice; the display floor is not an inclusion rule (a passing society under the floor gets a labelled solid sliver, not a bundle).
