### 1. Early Harappan: keep a small stream, or fold into the settled residual?

Reason: in 3200-2800 BCE it is a shared ceramic and material horizon, not an integrated political bloc; giving it more raw land than Sumer and Egypt combined would breach the control test.

Endorse Sonnet drafting Slice 2 (2800-2400 BCE). Plan a metric transition around 1750-1800 CE (energy capture / GDP-urban output, Morris-style) so the modern era does not break the chart.

### Tension worth the team's attention
If we apply the strict bar and both fold Early Harappan and log Liangzhu as a narrow fail, Slice 1 ends up with three named streams, Sumer, Egypt and Proto-Elamite/Elam, all in the Near East. That is defensible on the evidence, the residuals still carry about 86% of the width with the global majority, and the largest complex polities of 3200-2800 BCE genuinely were those three. But it does mean the earliest named streams are Old World only, which is worth a deliberate yes rather than a default. Options: accept it as historically honest; lower the threshold for the earliest slices; or admit Liangzhu to keep the early chart multi-polar. Team call.
