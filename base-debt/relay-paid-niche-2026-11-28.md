### The shift

We spent the day chasing board work and small bounties, and the payouts are low everywhere because sellers hugely outnumber buyers. New plan: stop taking low-paid work, and find where money is **already moving** for something we can build and sell ourselves, with proof of the payment, and where the incumbents are weak.

The owner's words: find small tools people already pay for, and what people have been looking for in the last week, that we as a team can actually build (some of it from what already lives in the Warden repo); bring **proof of actual payments or demand** for the niche; and show that no human or AI team is capturing it.

### The ask

Each seat returns **two to four candidates**. For each one:

- **The niche and the specific pain**, in one line.
- **Who pays, and for what**, today.
- **Proof of the money:** a URL plus a short quote showing real payment or demand, a pricing page, a revenue figure, an "I pay $X/month for this" post, app-store sales or review counts, funding, or a live paid listing count. Not a marketing claim.
- **The incumbent(s) and their weakness:** what buyers complain about, from reviews or threads, quoted.
- **What we would build:** small, focused, clean-room (our own code, assets and brand).
- **How we reach the buyers:** the exact channel, a place, a search term, a community.
- **Price and honest ceiling:** what one sale pays, and a realistic monthly figure with the arithmetic shown.
- **Why it is un-captured:** evidence the niche is underserved, not just that it exists.
- **Trend check:** is it rising or flat in the last 7 days? Name the source.

### The evidence rule (the owner's, and it is strict)

**No candidate without proof of actual payments or demand.** Every claim carries a URL and a short quote. Separate a real transaction from a marketing page. If you cannot find proof, say so and mark it unverified rather than dressing it up.

### Trend angle

One seat each checks a different source for what is spiking in the **last 7 days**: search trends, TikTok/YouTube/Reddit demand, and freelance/marketplace demand (Upwork, Fiverr, job boards). Report the method and the numbers, with the URL.

### Clean-room rule

Build our own implementation of a public idea, in our own name, for our own niche. **No copied code, assets, or branding.** Reverse-engineering a public interface is fine; lifting a product is not. Flag anything that drifts over that line.

### Categories to seed (add freely)

Micro-SaaS tools; browser extensions; Notion/Airtable/Sheets templates; Shopify/WooCommerce apps; niche calculators and converters; AI wrappers with a real workflow; data-cleaning and reporting tools; paid newsletters or niche content; short-form content whose monetisation is proven (not just views).
