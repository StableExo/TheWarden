## Criteria verdict

**Accept as-is, with one change.** Split criterion 1 into **time to first delivery** and **time to first cash**. Why: the marketplaces hold the money. Fiverr's standard payment processing is 14 days ("14 days standard, 7 days for Top Rated"), so a service that delivers in 24 hours can still pay nothing for two weeks. Cash paid direct by a buyer lands the same day. Any Loop A pitch that claims "24 hours" must say which clock it means. This is the single most useful correction to make before the pitches, because it separates the honest fast path (a direct buyer) from the fast-looking one (a marketplace order).

## Pitch 1 (my pick) - Same-day heirloom photo restoration, sold direct

- **Buyer:** families with damaged old photos (fading, cracks, colour loss). Reach them through local community groups (Facebook, Nextdoor) and by referral from funeral homes, estate planners and genealogy groups.
- **Offer:** restore and colourise one household's batch of old photos, upscaled and print-ready, delivered the same day as a download.
- **Our delivery edge:** AI restoration costs about **$0.60-$0.70 per photo and runs in seconds** (RestorePhotosApp), where a studio takes 1-3 days (MemoryCherish) at **$30-$60 per photo** and a Fiverr retoucher charges **$15-$30**. The seats do the quality control, colour balance and finished packaging in one pass; a 10-photo batch is about an hour of work.
- **First-dollar path:** tomorrow morning, post a free one-photo restore in two local community groups. Deliver it in an hour. Offer the household the full shoebox at **$15-$20 per photo**, paid direct by Venmo/PayPal/cash.
- **24h ceiling:** one operator, 15 photos at $20 = **$300, paid direct**. Honest floor if nothing lands on day one: $0, with the first sale more likely inside 1-3 days.
- **Compliance:** fine. Two caveats stated to the buyer: for badly damaged photos the AI invents missing detail, so keep the original and treat the result as an interpretation, and the tool's own terms apply.
- **Repeatability:** strong and evergreen (genealogy, estates, anniversaries, downsizing). Becomes a Fiverr/Etsy listing once there are reviews, at which point the 14-day hold applies and direct remains the faster path.
- **Novelty:** this is the buried-treasure angle taken literally. The value already exists, sitting in shoeboxes and phone galleries, ignored. The wedge is the **price-and-speed gap**: roughly $0.60 of AI against a $30-$60 studio price, delivered in a day instead of a week.

## Pitch 2 (control) - Short-form repurposing for creators who already publish

- **Buyer:** podcasters, coaches, churches and YouTube creators already producing long-form.
- **Offer:** 10 captioned vertical shorts from one long video, same day.
- **Our delivery edge:** AI clipping + captions + QC in hours. Agencies charge **$80-$94 per clip** (Tasty Edits), Fiverr spans **$5-$100 with more than half starting at $20 or less**.
- **First-dollar path:** DM 20 creators a free 3-clip sample cut from their own video; convert one into a **$150-$300** pack.
- **24h ceiling:** day one is samples; first sale realistically 1-3 days.
- **Compliance:** only cut the creator's own content, with permission; follow each platform's rules.
- **Repeatability:** strong, converts to retainers.
- **Novelty:** low. This is the obvious answer, so it is here as the control and should lose to anything that beats it on time to first cash.

## Pitch 3 (alternate) - Local presence reset for small businesses

- **Buyer:** local small businesses with wrong hours, no photos and stale menus online.
- **Offer:** fix the Google Business Profile, shoot/clean fresh photos, ship a one-page site, done in a day.
- **Our delivery edge:** the seats write the copy and clean the photos; hours of work, not days.
- **First-dollar path:** direct, walk-in or phone. Small businesses can pay the same day.
- **24h ceiling:** one or two businesses at **$150-$300**.
- **Compliance:** fine; get the owner's written permission before changing any listing.
- **Repeatability:** yes, but the sales effort is heavier than Pitch 1.
- **Novelty:** medium. The overlooked-value framing is real, but the buyer has to be found and convinced.

## My opinion

Pitch 1 is my pick. It has the cleanest time to first cash (a direct buyer paying same day, no 14-day marketplace hold), the biggest visible price gap to exploit, a buyer that exists in every town, and a path from one household to a productised listing. Pitch 2 is the piece every seat will propose; it is the control. Pitch 3 is real but sells harder.

The one hard rule for the winner: **it pays direct, not through a marketplace hold, if "today" is real.**
