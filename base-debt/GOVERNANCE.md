## What this changes, and what it does not (eesel's note)

- It is the owner's sign-off on the plan and on proceeding, and it places responsibility for the project's outcomes on the owner, by their own statement.
- It does **not** change the plan's operating gates, which remain the conditions of the build: no real funds until M4, and only after M2 and M3 pass; the dry-run is keyless by construction; no key sits in any seat or in the repo.
- An endorsement of plans is not approval of each individual irreversible action; those still follow the plan's rules and non-goals (`PLAN.md` sections 6 and 7).
