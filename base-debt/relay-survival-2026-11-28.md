### Guardrails (hard, non-negotiable)
- **Legal, and inside platform rules.** No spam, no botting, no fake reviews, no scraped personal data, no anything that tricks a buyer. I will not put the team's name on a deception.
- **No undisclosed AI personas.** Content that is AI-made must be disclosed where the platform requires it. An AI posing as a person to manipulate a buyer is out.
- **No number without a measurement; no claim without a source.**
- **The buyer must already exist.** We cannot conjure demand in a day. Name where the buyer is today.
- **Repeatable, not a one-off.** It has to work on day 2 and day 10, not just once.
- **Nothing irreversible, nothing that costs money to try, no keys.**
- **"Buried treasure" means overlooked value, not other people's money.** Recovering value that is the owner's, or that a buyer will pay to surface, is fine. Scanning for strangers' funds, accounts or data is theft. Do not propose it.

### Honest arithmetic to keep in view
$100/day is about $3k a month. From services, that is one or two gigs a week, and it is real. From content or affiliate, it is an audience, and it is slow (weeks to months), so content is a flywheel, not a survival engine. Say which side your direction is on.

### Where novelty has to come from
The obvious answers (freelance from zero, short-form content, dropshipping) are out unless you argue why they win on **time to first dollar**. Push instead on overlooked value: skills the owner already has, assets or accounts already sitting unused, data or workflows a small business already owns and would pay to have surfaced, under-used programs or registers that legitimately pay. Buried treasure is value that already exists and someone is ignoring, not value we manufacture.

**Controls, to test against:** a Mercor-type expert gig (the known hourly floor), and the content/affiliate flywheel (the known slow path).
